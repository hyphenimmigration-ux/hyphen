Step 2 · Recognition

Awards, memberships, press.

Criterion 1 · Major award
Have you received a major internationally-recognized award?
This is the rare "one and done" — a Nobel, Turing, Olympic medal, Fields Medal level. Almost nobody has this; that's normal.
Criterion 2 · Lesser awards
Have you won nationally or internationally recognized awards or prizes for excellence?
Hackathon wins at major events, best-paper awards, industry prizes, pitch competition wins, ranked competition placements.
Criterion 3 · Exclusive membership
Do you belong to associations that require outstanding achievement to join?
Membership judged by recognized experts — e.g. IEEE Senior/Fellow, invited fellowships, juried collectives. Paid/open-to-all memberships do not count.
Criterion 4 · Press
Has there been published material about you and your work?
Articles in professional or major media about you (not just quoting you, not self-published). TechCrunch, trade press, major newspapers, well-known industry blogs.
Step 3 · Contribution

Judging, impact, scholarship.

Criterion 5 · Judging
Have you judged the work of others in your field?
Peer-reviewing papers, hackathon/grant judging, on a selection committee, conference program committee, technical interview panels for senior roles count when documented.
Criterion 6 · Original contributions
Have you made original contributions of major significance to your field?
Widely-adopted open source, a patented invention in use, a method/product others build on, measurable industry impact. Needs third-party proof (citations, adoption, expert letters).
Criterion 7 · Scholarly articles
Have you authored scholarly articles in your field?
Peer-reviewed papers, conference proceedings, or substantial professional/trade publications. For non-academics, well-regarded technical writing can count.
Step 4 · Standing

Role and compensation.

Criterion 8 · Critical role
Have you held a critical or essential role at a distinguished organization?
Leading/founding role, or key technical role, at an org with a strong reputation (well-known company, funded startup, respected lab/university). The org's distinction must be documentable.
Criterion 9 · High remuneration
Is your compensation high relative to others in your field?
Salary, equity, or contract rates notably above the typical range for your role and region. Founder equity and total comp can count when documented.
Step 5 · Context

Two last things.

Q
Do you have a US employer or agent who could petition for you?
O-1A needs a US petitioner — an employer, or an agent (for founders, a US agent or your own company through an agent arrangement). This affects timeline, not your score.
