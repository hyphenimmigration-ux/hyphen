The honest NIW check

Know where you actually stand for the EB-2 NIW.

The National Interest Waiver lets you self-petition for a green card — no employer, no job offer, no labor certification — if you satisfy all three prongs of the Matter of Dhanasar test. Most people are told "maybe" with no real assessment. In ~7 minutes this gives you an honest score on each prong and the specific plan to strengthen the weak one.

Unlike the O-1A's "3 of 8" test, NIW requires all three prongs. A strong record cannot rescue a weak endeavor, and a great endeavor cannot rescue a weak position. This tool scores them honestly — gated by your weakest prong, exactly as USCIS adjudicates.

Step 1 · Prong 1 — Your endeavor

What are you actually proposing to do?

Q1 · Field
What field is your proposed endeavor in?
P1 · Substantial merit
How clearly defined and substantively meritorious is your specific endeavor?
NIW fails most often here. "I'm a good engineer" is not an endeavor. "I will develop X to solve Y" is. It must be specific.
P1 · National importance
Does your endeavor have importance beyond your employer or locality?
National/global implications — broad economic, scientific, health, security, or societal impact. Local or single-company benefit is not enough.
Step 2 · Prong 2 — Well-positioned

Are you the person to advance it?

P2 · Track record
What's your record of success in this area?
Publications with citations, funded work, adopted products, prior results, recognition. Evidence you've advanced similar work before.
P2 · Skills & qualifications
How well do your qualifications match this endeavor?
Advanced degree, specialized expertise, demonstrated skills directly relevant to advancing this specific work.
P2 · Progress & resources
Is there evidence of progress and a realistic path forward?
Funding, interest from stakeholders, a plan others find credible, momentum, institutional or investor backing.
Step 3 · Prong 3 — Why waive the requirement

Why is it in the US interest to skip the normal process?

P3 · Impracticality of labor cert
Would requiring a job offer & labor certification be impractical for you?
e.g. you're self-employed/a founder, your work is entrepreneurial or spans employers, or a single sponsoring job doesn't fit how the work happens.
P3 · Urgency / benefit of contribution
Is the benefit of your contribution urgent or significant enough to outweigh the protections labor certification provides?
USCIS weighs whether the US gains enough from waiving the process. Time-sensitive, high-impact, or scarce-expertise work strengthens this.
Context
Do you hold an advanced degree, or can you show exceptional ability?
EB-2 itself requires an advanced degree (or its equivalent) OR exceptional ability. This is the EB-2 gate before NIW even applies.
