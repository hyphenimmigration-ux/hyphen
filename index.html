The truth

You are not "not ready." You are a defined distance from ready.

"Talk to us when you're ready" is what immigration lawyers tell people they don't want to take on — because no one has ever defined what ready means.

USCIS has. The criteria are public. For most visas, eligibility is judged against a specific, written set of standards in the federal regulations. Hyphen reads your profile against those standards and tells you, with a real score on each one, where you genuinely stand.

If you're a 78, we say so. If you're a 34, we say that too. And in both cases, you get a concrete plan — what to build, in what order, to move the number. That's it. That's the product.

How it works

Three principles. That's the entire product.

01

Real criteria, not vibes

Every score is built on the published USCIS regulatory criteria for that visa. O-1A reads against the eight criteria in 8 CFR 214.2(o)(3)(iii). NIW reads against the three-prong Matter of Dhanasar framework. Your lawyer can inspect the model.

02

Calibrated to be honest

The math is tuned so 90+ is rare and earned — reserved for genuinely exceptional profiles. The bare regulatory minimum sits in the high-60s/low-70s ("in real range — now talk to an attorney"). Nothing inflated, nothing crushing.

03

The plan is the product

Every score ends with a ranked plan: the specific, achievable moves that close your gaps — fastest first. When you've genuinely crossed the line, the plan tells you to stop and talk to a real attorney, not stay in our product.

Trust & legal

What Hyphen is — and is not.

Immigration is sensitive. We hold a clear line on what this tool does, what it doesn't, and where you should go for actual legal help.

What we are

Honest information

A free, in-browser tool that reads your profile against published USCIS criteria and returns a calibrated strength score plus a specific plan. Built so you can see where you stand before deciding whether to invest in a petition.

What we are not

A law firm, or your lawyer

Hyphen does not provide legal advice, does not represent anyone before USCIS, and does not file petitions. The Score is informational strength guidance, not a prediction of outcome. For your specific case, you need a licensed immigration attorney.

What we don't store

Your data stays with you

Today, the Score runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not transmitted or stored on a server. When we add accounts (so you can save your profile across pathways), we will tell you exactly what we store, why, and how it's secured.
